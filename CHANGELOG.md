# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — Content Agent

- MIT growth playbooks in `skills/` for X, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit, Facebook, and Threads
- Content Agent drafts a post, comment/reply, or DM on the OpenRouter free model by default and saves it for approval
- Those drafts are never posted or sent
- Roadmap notes for v0.3 (sandboxed browser per AI employee) and v0.4 (named roles)

## 0.1.0 — first public release

- Business Profile drives discovery, qualification, scoring and drafts
- AI provider setup: OpenRouter (default, free models) or OpenAI
- Multi-inbox Gmail sender pool with per-inbox daily limits
- LinkedIn/X browser automation moved behind `ENABLE_SOCIAL_AUTOMATION` (off)
- Email compliance footer (sender, address, unsubscribe) on by default
